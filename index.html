Data model

Five ledgers, kept live — not a static database

Every screen above writes to, and reads from, these structured datasets.

MATERIAL

What was collected

  • Category, sub-category, description
  • Image reference, condition, source type
  • Approx. weight, estimated value
PRICE

What it's worth, where, when

  • Category, location, date/time
  • Buying vs. quoted price, unit
  • Recycler offer + historical trend
RECYCLER

Who can legally take it

  • Facility, location, materials accepted
  • Authorization status & registration
  • Offered rate, pickup, service area
TRANSACTION + TRACE

What happened to it next

  • Lot ID, collector ID, quoted vs. final price
  • Photos, GPS, timestamp, handover ref.
  • Recycler confirmation, payment status

Safety guidance

Shown, not just written

🔥🚫

No open cable burning

Releases toxic fumes and destroys recoverable copper value.तार जलाना नहीं — जहरीला धुआं और नुकसान

🧪🚫

No acid leaching of PCBs

Burns skin and lungs, and loses gold, gallium, tantalum to runoff.बोर्ड को एसिड में मत डालें

🔋⚠️

Handle batteries and CRTs carefully

Puncture or crushing risks fire and lead exposure — hand over sealed.बैटरी और CRT को सावधानी से रखें

Hindi and Marathi at launch, icon-first navigation, spoken price readouts for low-literacy users. English kept for recycler and admin-side interfaces.
